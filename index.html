Sandbox Concerts
One-off show concepts built around a specific artist, a specific room, and a specific feeling. Sandbox handles location scouting, production, promotion, and everything in between. Every show is designed so that the environment and the music are inseparable.

From black-tie productions to brand activation pop-ups, Sandbox builds events that people remember. Talent booking, staging, sound, lighting, photography, videography. The full production stack, applied to your brief.
